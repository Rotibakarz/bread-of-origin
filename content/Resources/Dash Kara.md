# Dash Kara
## What is a Dash Kara?
Dash Kara is a technique where you kara cancel the first 3 frames of a dash cancel into a special move.

![[dashkara_showcase.gif|250]]

Kara cancelling takes the startup properties of one move and applies them to the other, in this case we are taking the startup dash momentum of the dash cancel and applying it to our chant. 

This is incredibly useful for Asuka's combo optimizations and blockstrings as it keeps you close to the opponent without needing to spend extra resources, which for THE resource management character, is very helpful.

![[dashkara_no.gif|250]] ![[dashkara_yes.gif|250]]

In the first example, even though Asuka is `+5`, because he gets pushed out too far, he has to run back in, causing him to trade or even lose to Sol. In the second example, because the dash kara keeps Asuka close to Sol, Asuka can just immediately press `5K`, frametrapping Sol.
## How do I perform a Dash Kara?
As stated above, you want to quickly do a special move within a 3f window after the dash cancel. The trick here is to press `c.S/5K` and slightly delay a buffered dash macro, after that just immediately press your special button. You ideally want your buffered dash macro to come out on `[5]` after the `c.S/5K` then use your desired special. 

```
c.S/5K > dl dash > 236X/214X/22X/22HX
```

*You'll know if you've successfully performed it by the flames behind your feet.*
![[dashkara_flame.gif]]

It is also possible to delay your dash macro a little later by doing `c.S/5K > dl 2366X`, however you wouldn't be able to dashkara bookmark, recover mana and change testcase with that.

📝*Note: the 3f window only starts the moment your dash actually comes out, NOT the moment you press and buffer the dash button.*

**Aquila Safejump**
Ending a combo with Aquila Sets up for an auto-timed Fshiki Safejump.


**Inputs:**
``Aquila > Run Up (66) > c.S/2K > 2D > Recover Mana > rising 9j.P > instant j.D > jc j.K/land 2k``

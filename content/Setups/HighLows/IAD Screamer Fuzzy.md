# IAD Screamer Fuzzy
- After a throw/6H, 5P IAD screamer gives you a free fuzzy.
- If you manual time it by walking forward slightly and then IAD screamer, you can land behind the opponent, and you still have ur fuzzy. This can be made consistent with aquila. [Example](https://youtu.be/Y0NQlJqlC8k)
- It's a fake setup since the opponent can just crouch block or 6P it, a variation of this [[Lowpass IAD Screamer Fuzzy]] can't be 6P'ed.
- Regardless if its fake, since you are mostly doing this in safejump setups, it will absolutely catch people off guard.
- if you j.P anti air someone, you can manual time an air dash > screamer for the same fuzzy setup.

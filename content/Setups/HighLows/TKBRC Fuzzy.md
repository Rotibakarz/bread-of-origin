# TKBRC Fuzzy
Great OOM fuzzy to stall, the low 2K > 6H gives the better knockdown but if they block it you kinda die so 2K > 2K > 2D is far more consistent. Also if you go high, it's tk brc > j.S > jc j.K > j.K > 2K> 2D, during the second falling j.K you have to delay it so it doesn't hit too high or too low, definitely lab this a few times by recording yourself doing it and checking if it fully connects.

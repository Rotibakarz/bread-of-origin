# Setup Ranking System
Setups are ranked from 1 - 5 based on how useful they are.
- 5 - *Versatile setup that is highly recommended to learn.*
- 4 - *A solid setup to a*
- 3 - *A not so great Setup but great to add to your bag of tricks*
- 2 - *Usually a fake or niche mixup.*
- 1 - *Extremely Niche, not practical/difficult to use*

---
**1 to 5 scale ref**
5 indicates something that should or must be known for optimal play.
4 indicates something significantly important albeit not strictly necessary.
3 indicates something useful, often outclassed by another tech or having strong but niche use cases.
2 indicates something niche or not important, or most often, just something cheesy.
1 indicates something either so unbelievably niche its useless, or just useless
